## Glint 0.9.0-beta.12: play in your browser

**New**
- **Glint is now played on the website.** Press **Play** at glint.appwrite.network. There's nothing to download or install, and you always get the latest version.
- Solo games and the campaign now run on the Glint server, so you can pick up from any computer once you're signed in.

**Changed**
- The Windows download has been retired.
