## Glint 0.9.0-beta.8

**New**
- **A new Multiplayer screen.** Quick Match, **create a lobby** (public or private), **join with a code**, or pick from the list of open lobbies, which keeps itself up to date.
- **Public or private lobbies.** Public lobbies show up for everyone, and private ones need the code. The host can switch between them in the lobby.

**Changed**
- Glint always connects to the official server. There are no addresses to type, and no more "Host on this PC".
- The Account and Friends screens no longer show a server address.
