## Glint 0.9.0-beta.5: online play

Online games now run on a dedicated server, so you can play any time.

**New**
- **Quick Match.** One button puts you at a 4-player table with other players. It starts by itself, and bots fill any empty seats.
- **Rejoin.** If your connection drops or the game closes mid-round, a bot keeps your seat warm and the game reconnects you automatically. After a restart, the main menu offers *Rejoin*.
- **Chat** in lobbies and at the table. Press `Enter` or `T`. You can turn it off in Options.
- **Lobby tools.** Guests press *I'm ready* before the host can start. Hosts can hand over the host role or remove a player.

**Fixes**
- Dropped connections are noticed within seconds instead of leaving a frozen table.
