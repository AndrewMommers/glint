## Glint 0.9.0-beta.9

**Fixes**
- **Online play and accounts now reach the Glint server.** Builds since beta.5 were trying to connect to your own PC (`127.0.0.1`), so creating an account, signing in and online play failed. Update checks also never ran.

**Changed**
- Signing in or creating an account shows a popup with a spinner while it connects. If something goes wrong, the error appears in the form and what you typed stays put.
