## Glint 0.9.0-beta.4: the game is now **Glint** ✨

- **New name and look.** The game is now called Glint, with a new glass logo, app icon, splash screen and installer art.
- **New cards.** Every card has a faceted glass gem at its center, wild cards have a four-color gem, and card backs show a gem and a glint.
- **"GLINT!"** is the new call when you're down to one card. Press **G** (U still works). Forget it, and anyone can catch you for +2.
- **Fixed:** the end-of-round menu didn't appear after **Play again**, and replays didn't award XP. Both now work for every match.
- **Private lobbies:** when a round ends, the menu offers **Continue** or **Leave**.
- **Accounts** now run on a new cloud backend. Passwords need at least **8 characters**.
- New invite codes look like `GLINT-XXXX-XXXX`.

Upgrading from an earlier beta? Run the new installer. It replaces the old version, and your progress and settings carry over.
