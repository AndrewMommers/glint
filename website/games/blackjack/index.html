How to play

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

  1. 1

    Bet

    Click chips to build your bet, then press Bet. The hand deals when everyone has bet.

  2. 2

    Hit or stand

    Hit (H) for another card, Stand (S) to keep your total. Over 21 is a bust.

  3. 3

    Double or split

    Double (D) your bet for one more card, or Split (P) a pair into two hands.

  4. 4

    Beat the dealer

    The dealer draws to 17. A blackjack (ace + ten-card) pays 3 to 2, a win pays 1 to 1.

FAQ

Is this real gambling?

No. There's no real money involved at all: chips are free, have no value, and can't be bought, sold or cashed out.

What are the table rules?

Six-deck shoe, reshuffled when it runs low. Blackjack pays 3 to 2 and the dealer stands on all 17s and peeks for blackjack. You can double on any first two cards (also after splitting) and split up to four hands.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a table and share its 4-letter code, invite friends from your friends list, or jump into a Quick Match. Everyone plays their own hands against the dealer.

Do I need an account?

No, guests get 1,000 chips at each table. A free account keeps your chips, adds the daily bonus and works across all Glint games.
