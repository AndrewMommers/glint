FAQ

Is Glint free?

Yes. Every game in the library is free to play.

Do the chip games use real money?

No. Blackjack and Texas Hold'em use free play chips that have no cash value and can't be bought or cashed out.

What do I need to play?

A computer with an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and an internet connection. Phones and tablets aren't supported yet.

Do I need an account?

Not to play solo. A free account lets you play online, add friends and keep your progress on any computer.

What data do you collect?

For online play: your username, game stats and progress. If you send feedback from a game, that's stored too, along with the game log if you choose to attach it. Nothing is sold or shared.
