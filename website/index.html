How to play

If you've ever played a shedding card game, you already know most of it.

  1. 1

    Match the pile

    Play a card that matches the top card by color, number or symbol, or play a wild.

  2. 2

    Can't play? Draw

    Draw a card. If it fits, you can play it straight away.

  3. 3

    Call GLINT!

    About to play your second-to-last card? Press G. Forget, and anyone can catch you for +2.

  4. 4

    Empty your hand

    First to play their last card wins the round and scores the cards left in everyone else's hands.

FAQ

Is Glint free?

Yes. The closed beta is free to download and play.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) for now. Other platforms may come later.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

The beta isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Always download from the official releases page.

Do I need an account?

Not for singleplayer or the campaign. An account (with a beta invite code) lets you play online, add friends and sync your progress.

What data do you collect?

For online play: your username, game stats and progress. If you send feedback from the game, that's stored too, along with the game log if you choose to attach it. Nothing is sold or shared.

How do I report a bug?

Use Feedback in the main menu. Your game version and log are attached automatically, which helps a lot.
