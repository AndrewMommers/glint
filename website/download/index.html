Installing

  1. 1

    Run the installer

    Open Glint-Setup.exe from your downloads. No admin rights needed.

  2. 2

    "Windows protected your PC"?

    The beta isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Play

    Launch Glint from the Start menu. Solo and campaign work offline, and online play needs an invite code.

  4. 4

    Updating

    Nothing to download again. Glint updates itself from inside the game, and your progress and settings are kept.

Verify the download (SHA-256)

In PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\Glint-Setup.exe
