UNO Glass - Closed Beta

Thanks for helping test UNO Glass!

- This is a confidential beta build. Please don't share the installer or
  your invite code.
- To play online, create an account in the game (Main menu -> Sign in /
  create account) using the invite code you were sent.
- Found a bug or have an idea? Use "Feedback" in the main menu. Your game
  version and log are attached automatically.
- New builds: the game tells you when an update is required. Just run the
  new installer; your progress is kept.

Singleplayer and the Campaign work offline.
